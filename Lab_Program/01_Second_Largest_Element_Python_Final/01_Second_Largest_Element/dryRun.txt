PROGRAM 01 - DRY RUN

INPUT
Array = [12, 7, 25, 18, 10]

STEP 1
Divide into:
[12, 7, 25] and [18, 10]

STEP 2
Compare 12 and 7
Winner = 12
Defeated by 12 = [7]

Compare 12 and 25
Winner = 25
Defeated by 25 = [12, 7]

STEP 3
Compare 18 and 10
Winner = 18
Defeated by 18 = [10]

STEP 4
Final comparison:
25 vs 18
Winner = 25

Elements directly defeated by 25:
[12, 18]

STEP 5
Compare 12 and 18
Winner = 18

Therefore:
Second largest = 18

COMPARISON COUNT
12 vs 7  = 1
12 vs 25 = 1
18 vs 10 = 1
25 vs 18 = 1
12 vs 18 = 1

Total = 5 comparisons

OBSERVATION
The algorithm does not sort the complete array. It only examines the elements that directly lost to the maximum.

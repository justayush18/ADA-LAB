PROGRAM 04 - LEARNING REFLECTION

This experiment helped me understand recursive problem decomposition through the Tower of Hanoi.

The problem follows T(n) = 2T(n-1) + 1 and requires exactly 2^n - 1 moves. Therefore, its time complexity is exponential.

The compact recursive implementation clearly represents the three main operations: moving n-1 disks, moving the largest disk, and moving the remaining n-1 disks.

I also understood that recursion uses additional stack space and that the recursion depth is proportional to the number of disks.

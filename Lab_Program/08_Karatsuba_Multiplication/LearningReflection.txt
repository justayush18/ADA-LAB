PROGRAM 08 - LEARNING REFLECTION

This experiment helped me understand how Karatsuba multiplication improves multiplication by reducing the number of recursive multiplications.

I learned that the algorithm uses divide and conquer and calculates the middle term from three recursive products instead of four.

I also learned that theoretical complexity and actual execution time can differ because of recursion overhead and optimized built-in operations.

PROGRAM 06 - LEARNING REFLECTION

This experiment helped me understand that pivot selection can significantly affect Quick Sort performance.

The same Quick Sort procedure can behave differently depending on whether the first, last, middle, or random element is selected as the pivot.

Balanced partitions generally lead to O(n log n), while highly unbalanced partitions can lead to O(n²).

Testing sorted, reverse-sorted, and random datasets helped me understand the importance of choosing an appropriate pivot strategy.
